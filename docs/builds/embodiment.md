# Build notes: a body he learns, not one he reads (2026-11-05)

Research record. Design summary in `docs/architecture.md` (pattern 10).

## The problem

Every action carried exact gains, the body always took the best score, and
the brain was told the numbers. The creator: "He'll have needs but not know
what they are for or how to fill them, he'll have to try things out and see
what they do and evaluate how he feels after. We don't get +20 energy from
something."

## What changed

- **Physiology (hidden):** digestion (about a third of a meal felt at once,
  the rest over 40 to 90 minutes), variation between meals (plus or minus
  20%), thirst (faster in heat and when active; dehydration tires him),
  soreness from tricks (heals, faster asleep), upset stomach, getting wet
  (dries under shelter), stiffness from sitting still, skill (practice makes
  tricks land; tired or sore, they fail more).
- **Sensation:** felt levels with a slowly wandering bias and a little
  noise; at most four percepts a moment, in words with a strength word;
  absorbed in making something, mild hunger goes unnoticed; thirst can read
  as an empty stomach.
- **Learning (mind):** beliefs from experience, else from the kind of thing,
  else an instinct's hunch, else nothing (plus a curious pull toward a couple
  of untried things). A second look an hour later for slow effects. Surprise
  measured; a big one becomes a thought. He also learns from what his body did
  while his mind was elsewhere.
- **Learning (body):** habits, including bad ones remembered ("a body that
  ate a pen remembers"). With no habit, a few instincts, and near critical, a
  rescue that uses what the world really offers. That rescue is the one place
  the truth is used, and the habit it forms is real.
- **Verbs:** thirteen, each with an animation. Any verb can be tried on
  anything near him; the outcome comes from the thing's properties.
- **Pushback:** the vending machine jams and a thump frees it; fountain water
  is sometimes stale; tricks fail; rain soaks him.

## What the simulated month found

- **Starving while his body went for food:** his mind only learned from its
  own choices, so it never learned what food does. Fixed by letting him learn
  from his body's doings too, and by letting the body win in an emergency.
- **Credit for a meal still settling:** a fuller stomach after something else
  is now put down to the meal.
- **Re-deciding money every moment:** decided money stays decided.
- **Habits back to back:** three times in two hours and he thinks instead.

After the fixes, three simulated days: every check passes, no pass-outs, his
time spread across drawing, his shop, the mirror, writing, code and his paper.
On the live town, his body learned its first habits within an hour, all from
play: the mirror, the shelf, the bench.
